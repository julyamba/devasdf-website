Helpline Track

Live SOS console for response stations

The other half of Helpline's SOS feature. When someone asks for help in Helpline, nearby response stations see the request on a live map, route to the person, talk with them by text or voice, and mark it resolved when help arrives.

  • Live map with instant SOS alerts
  • Driving route and ETA
  • Text and voice replies
  • Alert radius from 1 to 50 km
Request station access
  1. Someone asks for helpThey tap SOS in Helpline. Their location and details go to nearby stations.
  2. A station is alertedHelpline Track sounds an alarm and pins the request on the map.
  3. Help arrivesResponders route, reply and resolve the request.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Where can I download your apps?

Helpline is available now on Google Play. Helpline Track is for response stations and is available on request. Dejadot can be downloaded as an APK from its release page, and WayBack is in early access and coming soon.

What is Helpline Track?

Helpline Track is the console that response stations use to receive SOS requests sent from Helpline. It isn't a public download: stations get an account from us. If you run a rescue, medical or security team and want to receive Helpline SOS requests in your area, email us.

Are your apps free?

Yes. Dejadot, Helpline and WayBack are free to download, with no ads. If we ever add paid features, we'll say so clearly on the store listing first.

Do you collect my personal data?

As little as possible. Dejadot and WayBack keep everything on your phone. Helpline sends your location to our server to find emergency contacts near you, and if you send an SOS, your details go to nearby response stations. Our Privacy Policy lists every detail, app by app.

Which platforms do you support?

Android first. Some of our apps are built with cross-platform tools, and iOS versions are on our roadmap.

Can you build an app for my business?

We take on a small number of partner projects each year, especially mobile apps, games and offline or location-based tools. Email us with a short description and we'll reply.

I found a bug or have an idea. Where do I send it?

Email hello@devasdf.xyz with the app name, your phone model and what happened. Screenshots help a lot.

How do I delete my data?

For apps that store data only on your phone, uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it. For anything else, email us and we'll handle it within 30 days. See the data deletion section.
